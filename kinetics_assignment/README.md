# Reaction kinetics

Assignment on reaction kinetics, drug stability and shelf life.

Students first determine the reaction order from concentration-time data by comparing linearized fits, and turn the slope of the best fit into a rate constant. They then use the rate constant to calculate the time to reach a given fraction remaining (shelf life) and the amount remaining after a given time. Finally, they fit Arrhenius plots to rate constants measured at several temperatures and pH values, estimate the activation energy, and extrapolate the rate constant to a new temperature.
