# Vapour Pressure

In this activity, students work with vapour pressure measured across a range of temperatures either from a provided dataset or from their own lab measurements. The R guide walks through turning that data into a Clausius–Clapeyron plot (a linearized form of the underlying non-linear relationship), reading the enthalpy and entropy of vaporization ($\Delta_{vap}H$, $\Delta_{vap}S$) off the fitted line, and from there calculating each solvent's standard and normal boiling point.

## Background: the wet-lab exercise

The same Clausius–Clapeyron analysis is also applied in SDU's physical lab exercise for the course (Øvelse A3, "Damptryk af en lavtkogende væske" — vapour pressure of a low-boiling liquid), where students measure the vapour pressure of diethyl ether and methylene chloride (dichloromethane) at 8–10 temperatures, then determine $\Delta_{vap}H$, the normal boiling point, and $\Delta_{vap}S$ for each liquid and check the results against Trouton's rule.
